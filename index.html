Our method

The Signature Profile.™

Every studio that has ever been burned by an editor was burned by the same thing: the film came back technically fine and emotionally wrong. It wasn't theirs.

So we don't start with your footage. We start with your work. We watch three to five of your recent films and write down how you cut — average clip length, how long you hold a moment, how warm you grade, what music you reach for, how you open and how you land. That document becomes the brief for every edit we deliver, and you get a copy.

It means the first film we cut for you is built from evidence instead of guesswork — and the tenth still sounds like you, even if a different editor cuts it.

FAQ

The questions we always get.

Anything not here, ask on the call — we'd rather answer it properly than write a paragraph about it.

Will the films actually look like mine?

That's the entire reason the Signature Profile exists. We watch three to five of your recent films and write down how you cut before we open your footage — clip lengths, how long you hold a moment, your colour, your music, how you open and close. Every edit is checked against that document before it reaches you. And on the first film revisions are unlimited: we keep going until it reads as yours. If it never does, you don't pay and you keep the files.

What does "you only pay if you deliver it" actually mean?

Exactly what it says. Send us one wedding. We edit it and send it back. If you pass our cut to your couple, you pay the invoice. If you decide not to use it, you owe nothing — and the files, project file included, are still yours. No deposit, no contract, no minimum. We take the risk because we'd rather show you the work than describe it.

How long does it take?

Ten working days as standard, from the moment the footage finishes uploading. Multi-day and cultural weddings run 10–14 days because there is simply more of it. Rush delivery under seven days is available at +40% when we have the capacity — and we will tell you honestly when we don't, rather than take the job and miss it.

How do I send you the footage?

However you already work. Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer, MASV, Frame.io — or a hard drive if that is genuinely easier. We fit into your workflow. Asking a studio to change how it moves files in order to hire an editor is backwards.

Do you handle South Asian and other multi-day weddings?

Yes, and it is some of our strongest work. A mehndi, a sangeet, a ceremony and a reception is not one wedding with more footage — it is four films sharing an arc, and an editor who does not know the running order produces something technically clean and emotionally wrong. Ours know which moments carry the weight and which can be compressed. Sikh, Muslim and Hindu ceremonies included.

Is my couples' footage safe with you?

Yes. Every project sits in access-controlled storage, only the editor assigned to your account and the reviewer can open it, and nothing is ever posted, shown or used as a sample without your written permission — every film on this site is shared with the client's permission. We are a white-label partner: your clients should never know we exist, and that discretion is part of what you are paying for.

Where is your team based?

Our editing team works in-house, and our client contact runs on Cyprus time — which is how we cover both US and UK studios inside their working day. It is also, frankly, why we are strong on multi-day cultural weddings: our editors grew up around them. We say this openly because you will find out anyway, and a partner who is cagey about where they sit is not a partner worth having.

How do we start?

Book a twenty-minute call, or message us on WhatsApp. We will talk about what you shoot and how you cut, then build your Signature Profile within 48 hours and send it over free — yours to keep either way. If you like it, send one wedding on the pay-only-if-you-use-it basis. That is the whole onboarding.
